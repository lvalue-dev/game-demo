## 1. Korean game market size and platform split (KOCCA 대한민국 게임백서), indie scene size and typical revenue

### Takeaway
The latest white paper (2025 edition, published 2026-03-25, covering calendar 2024) puts the Korean game market at KRW 23.85 trillion with mobile at 59.0%, PC 25.2% and console 5.0%; mobile is by far the largest but the slowest-changing, and half of all Korean game companies earn under about KRW 0.83 billion a year. No indie-only revenue statistics were found.

### Cited Findings
- 2024 Korean game industry revenue was KRW 23.8515 trillion, up 3.9% year on year, a record high (2025 게임백서, published 2026-03-25) — [스포츠경향](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202603251522003)
- 2024 platform split: mobile KRW 14.0710 trillion (59.0%), PC KRW 6.0094 trillion (25.2%), console KRW 1.1836 trillion (5.0%), arcade KRW 275.9 billion (1.2%). Mobile share was 57.4% in 2020 and 58.8% in 2022 — [Korea Business Review](https://www.koreabizreview.com/articles/kbr-analysis-kbr-research-notes-23-8515-20260802-1ktg)
- 2024 growth by platform: console +4.8%, mobile +3.4%, PC +2.0%, arcade -3.2%. Exports were USD 8.50346 billion, +1.3% — [아시아경제](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026032515223006138)
- The 2025 white paper covers 816 game production/distribution companies in 2024: mean revenue KRW 24.952 billion, median KRW 831 million, i.e. half of Korean game companies earn under about KRW 0.8 billion (이데일리, 2026-04-14) — [Daum/이데일리](https://v.daum.net/v/20260414181500637)
- The previous edition (2024 white paper, 2023 data) also had mobile at about 60% of revenue, followed by PC and console — [쿠키뉴스](https://www.kukinews.com/article/view/kuk202503170038)
- Sensor Tower: Korean mobile game revenue in 2H 2025 was USD 2.8 billion, +8.3% versus 1H 2025; Nexon became the #1 publisher for the first time — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/ko/blog/2H2025-mobile-games-recap-in-Korea)
- Sensor Tower's 2025 Korea report headline: the Korean mobile game market earned about KRW 7.6 trillion in 2025; mobile game digital ad spend in Jan-Sep 2025 was 12 times PC/console game ad spend — [디지털 인사이트](https://ditoday.com/?p=136138)
- Headlines from late 2025 to 2026 report falling game participation overall, with PC and console usage rates rising while mobile usage fell (headline only; underlying survey figures not retrieved) — [CEO스코어데일리 2025-12-24](https://www.ceoscoredaily.com/page/view/2025122416210475424); [바이라인네트워크 2026-01](https://byline.network/2026/01/6-315/)
- Sensor Tower 2026 Korea report: Korean mobile downloads fell while revenue held and revenue per download hit a record — [게임메카](https://www.gamemeca.com/view.php?gid=1782789); [디지털 인사이트](https://ditoday.com/?p=156271)

### Inferences
- The four platform shares sum to 90.4%; the remainder is venue revenue (PC방 and arcade venues), which the white paper counts in the total. The source extract did not state this, so treat it as my reading of the white paper's usual structure.
- A 59% mobile share does not mean mobile is the accessible segment for a newcomer: that revenue is concentrated in a few large publishers (see section 3), while PC and console are the segments where usage is growing.
- The KRW 831 million median is for registered companies, most of them larger than a solo developer; a first-time indie should expect to be well below it.

### Gaps
- The 2026 white paper (2025 data) had not appeared in search results; it is normally published in the first quarter of the following year.
- No KOCCA indie-specific survey (number of indie teams, average team size, typical revenue, share releasing on Steam) was found.
- White paper forecasts for 2025/2026 and the PC-package versus PC-online split were not retrieved.

## 2. Korean players on Steam, Korean indie hits and the mobile-to-Steam shift

### Takeaway
Small Korean teams have produced verified Steam hits in 2023-2026 (Shape of Dreams: 4 people, 1.3M+ copies; Skul: 2M+), and indie pipelines (BIC, KOCCA Dev Camp, IndieCraft, G-STAR) all grew sharply in 2026 and are increasingly tied to Steam, but I found no reliable figure for Korea's share of Steam users or revenue.

### Cited Findings
- Shape of Dreams (developer Lizard Smoothie, publisher Neowiz; released September 2025) passed 500,000 cumulative global sales about two weeks after launch (announced 2025-09-30) — [디지털데일리](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2025093017143501389)
- Shape of Dreams reached 1 million copies on 2025-12-31, about three and a half months after launch, making it Neowiz's second million-selling indie after Skul; sales by region were about 60% Asia and about 30% North America/Europe; the team is four people (two co-leads plus two artists) — [디스이즈게임](https://www.thisisgame.com/articles/410742) (attribution uncertain between this and [게임메카](https://www.gamemeca.com/en/mv.php?gid=1770323))
- Shape of Dreams was reported at over 1.3 million cumulative sales in June 2026 — [디지털데일리](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2026061714475215853)
- Neowiz signed the Shape of Dreams publishing contract in January 2025, eight months before release — [바이라인네트워크](https://byline.network/2025/01/13-422)
- Skul: The Hero Slayer was the first Korean indie to pass 1 million copies and has sold over 2 million; SANABI (Wonder Potion, published by Neowiz, released 2023-11) raised over KRW 1.4 billion in merchandise crowdfunding — [비즈워치 2025-07-29](https://news.bizwatch.co.kr/article/mobile/2025/07/29/0031)
- SANABI has no official sales figure in the results. Third-party estimators disagree by almost 4x: about 593,000 units (SteamData.AI), about 859,000 units as of 2026-08-03 (Raijin), about 2.1-2.2 million (Gamerevenuedata). Steam rating is 97% positive on 42,000+ reviews — [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/1562700/SANABI); [SteamData.AI](https://steamdata.ai/game/1562700); [Gamerevenuedata](https://gamerevenuedata.pages.dev/games/sanabi/)
- A This Is Game column on Korean indie games notable on Steam in 2025 says more than 100 Korean-made games were visible on Steam in 2025. Examples released in 2025 without big-studio ties: 숲속의 작은 마녀 (Sunny Side Up; early access 2022, full release September 2025, 10,000+ user reviews), 레토피아 (Cassel Games, May 2025), 넌홀리 (Chowbie, 1,000+ reviews), 몰티즈와 복실복실 온천 (Sinkhole Studio). The list is one writer's selection from SteamDB review counts, not sales — [디스이즈게임](https://www.thisisgame.com/articles/410373)
- 40% of games released on Steam in 2025 did not recoup the USD 100 listing fee — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-49120)
- G-STAR 2026's indie showcase is its largest ever (114 indie developers per one report, 106 per another) and adds a G-STAR special promotion page and featured banner inside Steam — [바이라인네트워크](https://byline.network/2026/10/28-685/); [아이뉴스24](https://inews24.com/view/2019941)
- A KOCCA publication quotes an expert saying Steam's position is unmatched for developers because anyone can list freely (2023, older) — [KOCCA](https://www.kocca.kr/global/2023_7+8/sub01_03.html)

### Inferences
- The verified Korean small-team hits share a pattern: action/roguelite or strongly narrative games with distinctive 2D art, a Korean publisher (mostly Neowiz), early access or long demo exposure, and a majority of sales from Asia. Shape of Dreams' 60% Asia split suggests Korean, Chinese and Japanese localization matters more than for a Western indie.
- Record application counts in 2026 (BIC +44%, Dev Camp more than double, IndieCraft record) show more Korean indies entering, mostly on PC/Steam; that is also more competition for the same festival slots and grants.
- Third-party Steam sales estimates are unreliable at the level of a single game (SANABI ranges from 0.6M to 2.2M); only publisher-announced numbers should be quoted as fact.

### Gaps
- Korea's share of Steam users or revenue: not found. Results only gave global figures (about 140M monthly users in 2025; Chinese-language users over 50% per one outlet).
- No statistic was found that directly measures Korean indies moving from mobile to Steam; the trend is supported only by the festival/grant counts above and commentary.
- Sales for Unsouled and other one-person Korean titles were not found. 30XX is a US game (Batterystaple), not Korean.
- Typical (median) revenue of a Korean indie Steam release was not found.

## 3. Korean mobile market for small developers; ONE store and Galaxy Store terms

### Takeaway
Korean mobile revenue is concentrated in large publishers and, increasingly, Chinese 4X strategy titles, and Korean publishers themselves are growing overseas rather than at home; ONE store offers the cheapest domestic terms (20% base, 10% for small developers, 5% with own payment) but is small and loss-making. I found no hard UA-cost figures.

### Cited Findings
- Chinese 4X strategy games have ended the MMORPG-only dominance of Korea's mobile top-grossing charts, and Chinese companies now hold top-grossing positions (2026 reporting) — [바이라인네트워크 2026-03-18](https://byline.network/2026/03/18-552/); [뉴스토마토](https://newstomato.com/ReadNews.aspx?no=1318643)
- A June 2026 series describes Korean games as squeezed by OTT/video on the time side and by Chinese titles on the revenue side — [Daum 2026-06-26](https://v.daum.net/v/20260626063201145)
- Sensor Tower 2026 Korea report: for the top 10 Korean mobile publishers, overseas in-app revenue rose 92% year on year in Q1-Q3 2026 while domestic revenue rose 8%; overseas share of Korean publishers' downloads rose from 88% to 92% — [토요경제](https://sateconomy.co.kr/news/view/1065596230625861)
- Mobile game digital ad spend in Korea was 12 times PC/console game ad spend in Jan-Sep 2025 (Sensor Tower) — [디지털 인사이트](https://ditoday.com/?p=136138)
- ONE store cut its commission from 30% to 20% in 2018 and charges 5% when the developer uses its own payment system — [디지털데일리 2026-02-02](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2026020209560264367)
- ONE store's 50% reduction for small developers (monthly transactions of USD 5,000 or less, giving a 10% rate) was previously renewed yearly and was made a permanent policy in February 2026 — [디지털데일리 2026-02-02](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2026020209560264367)
- ONE store launched a web shop product ("원웹샵") in April 2026 priced at 8% including payment-gateway fees and hosting, positioned as about three times cheaper than Google/Apple — [MTN 2026-04-30](https://news.mtn.co.kr/news-detail/2026043014113930615)
- ONE store's 2025 operating revenue was KRW 113.3 billion with a KRW 9.7 billion operating loss — [뉴스토마토](https://newstomato.com/ReadNews.aspx?no=1319265)
- An August 2025 report says ONE store overtook Apple to become Korea's #2 app market (headline only; metric not retrieved) — [바이라인네트워크 2025-08-14](https://byline.network/2025/08/14-486/)
- Large Korean mobile publishers are moving to their own payment systems to avoid store fees — [뉴스토마토](https://newstomato.com/ReadNews.aspx?no=1302152)

### Inferences
- The mobile ad-spend ratio (12x PC/console) and the dominance of well-funded 4X/RPG publishers imply that paid user acquisition, not store featuring, decides Korean mobile charts. A solo developer without a UA budget is unlikely to chart on Google Play or the App Store in Korea.
- ONE store's low fee helps margin only if there is revenue; with its scale and losses it is a secondary channel, not a primary launch target.

### Gaps
- No CPI/UA cost figures for Korea were found.
- No quantified Chinese share of Korea's top-grossing charts (e.g. number of Chinese titles in the Google Play top 10) was found; only qualitative reporting.
- Galaxy Store commission and terms: nothing found.
- Whether ONE store's 10% small-developer rate stacks with the 5% own-payment rate was not confirmed.

## 4. Regulation: ratings, loot boxes, domestic agent, law revisions, shutdown, privacy

### Takeaway
Rating is still legally required for games distributed in Korea: mobile games are self-rated through Google/Apple/ONE store, while Steam (Valve) is not a designated self-rating operator, so a Korea-based developer selling a PC game to Koreans is expected to get a rating from the private board GCRB, which is opening a simplified questionnaire route for PC/console in late 2026. Mandatory pre-rating has not been abolished; the full rewrite of the Game Industry Act was still at the debate stage in August 2026, and a narrower amendment passed on 2026-10-01.

### Cited Findings
**Rating system**
- The Game Industry Promotion Act requires a rating for games distributed in Korea, except youth-accessible games on open markets that are self-rated (2020 fact-check, older) — [뉴스포스트](https://www.newspost.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=79330)
- Valve is not a designated self-rating operator (자체등급분류사업자). In 2020 GRAC sent rating guidance to overseas developers on Steam, called it guidance rather than enforcement, and said blocking or region-locking was not under discussion (2020, older) — [뉴스핌](https://m.newspim.com/news/view/20200605000188); [이데일리](https://www.edaily.co.kr/news/read?newsId=04414886622386256&mediaCodeNo=257)
- In July 2024 Steam was reported to be reviewing whether to obtain Korean self-rating operator status — [네이트뉴스 2024-07-03](https://news.nate.com/view/20240703n31844)
- GCRB (게임콘텐츠등급분류위원회), a private body under the Game Culture Foundation, has rated all-ages/12/15 PC and console games since 2014. GRAC announced on 2025-10-31 that adult-rated (청소년이용불가) rating work would also move to the private body from November 2025, with the delegation period running to 2030-05-22. Gambling-simulation games (go-stop, poker) and arcade stay with GRAC — [디지털데일리 2025-10-31](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2025103115000279592)
- A 2026-11-03 report says PC and console developers will be able to use simplified review (a questionnaire plus a pledge of responsibility, giving a fast rating; used mostly by small developers) at GCRB for all-ages to 15+ games — [비즈워치 2026-11-03](https://news.bizwatch.co.kr/article/mobile/2026/11/03/0019)
- At the October 2025 National Assembly audit, the PC/console rating fee was cited as KRW 3.24 million before VAT (KRW 3.564 million with VAT) per game, with no refund on rejection and 75% of the fee charged again for re-review — [디지털데일리 2025-10-23](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2025102314322989663) (attribution uncertain)
- A Korean solo developer's October 2026 cost write-up gives a much lower figure, KRW 360,000-720,000 plus VAT, for rating a Steam game, and USD 100 for the Steam fee — [NEONVOID blog](https://icenovel.com/projects/neonvoid/neonvoid-steam-registration)
- Rating fees are computed from a per-platform base amount multiplied by coefficients for network play, genre and Korean-language support (older fee structure description) — [게임메카](https://www.gamemeca.com/view.php?gid=261554)
- Smilegate's Stove is a designated self-rating operator and was re-designated for another five years (reported 2026-03-03); Stove Indie and the Epic Games Store do not charge developers a rating fee, and Stove covers the fee for adult-rated titles that need outside review — [디지털데일리 2026-03-03](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2026030311103874486); [Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/Hs8koMcpJh); [폴리뉴스](https://www.polinews.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=509544)
- Rated games must file a content-modification report within 24 hours of an update; experts listed this as needing reform at an August 2026 forum — [데일리안](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1675609)

**Law revisions 2025-2026**
- A full-revision bill (lead sponsor 조승래, September 2025) would rename the law to the "Act on Promotion of Game Culture and Industry", create a game promotion agency, split digital games from venue-based games, and make private self-rating the default for digital games while keeping gambling-type and web-board games under separate control. It was still being debated at expert forums in April and August 2026 — [데일리안](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1675609); [뉴스토마토](https://www.newstomato.com/ReadNews.aspx?no=1309506); [이투데이](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2611913)
- A partial amendment passed the National Assembly plenary on 2026-10-01 (committee alternative adopted 2026-09-16). It adds a duty to protect domestic users and handle complaints, lets authorities order network access to be refused/suspended/restricted when a corrective order on probability disclosure is ignored, tightens domestic-agent rules (an overseas company with a Korean subsidiary must designate that entity), and allows document requests. It takes effect six months after promulgation — [법률신문](https://www.lawtimes.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=228326); [율촌](https://www.yulchon.com/ko/resources/publications/newsletter-view/45445/page.do); [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000337901)

**Loot boxes (확률형 아이템)**
- Probability disclosure has been mandatory since March 2024. From March 2024 to September 2025, 338 game companies committed 2,181 labelling violations (GRAC data released via lawmaker 김승수, reported February 2026) — [딜사이트](https://dealsite.co.kr/articles/156564) (attribution uncertain)
- Of 261 violations found in March-June of the first year, over 60% came from overseas companies (59 companies) — [디지털데일리 2025-01-06](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2025010614495495712)
- Since 2025-08-01, a company that intentionally violates probability disclosure can be ordered to pay up to three times the damage, and the burden of proving no intent or negligence is on the company; GRAC set up a reporting and relief centre — [CEO스코어데일리 2025-08-04](https://www.ceoscoredaily.com/page/view/2025080415181654698); [한국경제](https://www.hankyung.com/article/2025072995561)

**Domestic agent (국내대리인)**
- In force since 2025-10-23. It applies to overseas game distributors/providers with no Korean address or office that had prior-year revenue of KRW 1 trillion or more, or a game with 1,000 or more average daily mobile installs in Korea in the prior year, or a reported incident; the fine for not designating is up to KRW 20 million — [디지털데일리 2025-10-14](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2025101417144080303)
- As of July 2026, 80 of 81 obligated overseas companies had designated an agent, and 24 more did so voluntarily (104 total) — [디지털데일리 2026-07-10](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/m/page/view/2026071017091184928)

**Shutdown law**
- The compulsory midnight shutdown was abolished on 2022-01-01 and replaced by the opt-in game-time selection system, under which a minor under 18 or a guardian can request restricted hours (2021-2022 reporting, older) — [디지털데일리 2022-01-05](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2022010511073278654); [뉴시스 2021-08-25](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20210825_0001559787)

### Inferences
- The domestic-agent rule does not apply to a Korea-based developer (it targets companies with no Korean address); it matters only as a sign that enforcement against unrated or non-compliant foreign titles is tightening.
- A premium PC game with no loot boxes avoids the probability-disclosure regime entirely. Any gacha or random-reward monetization on mobile brings disclosure duties, monitoring and, since August 2025, 3x damages exposure.
- For a Korean resident selling on Steam with Korean language support, the practical route is: 게임제작업 registration, then a GCRB rating (simplified review if all-ages to 15+), then enter the rating in Steamworks. Launching on Stove Indie additionally gives a free self-rating path. This sequence is my synthesis; the exact GCRB fee for a small indie title needs checking, since sources range from about KRW 0.36 million to KRW 3.24 million.
- The amendment passed on 2026-10-01 would take effect around spring 2027 if promulgated in October-November 2026; the promulgation date was not confirmed.

### Gaps
- Whether Valve has applied for or received self-rating operator status as of November 2026: no confirmation found; latest hard data point is the July 2024 "reviewing" report.
- Whether GRAC has taken enforcement action against unrated Steam games by Korean developers in 2024-2026: nothing found.
- Current GCRB fee table and processing time, and whether an individual (not a business) can apply: not retrieved.
- PIPA (개인정보보호법) specifics for games: no source retrieved. Unverified background to check: consent of a legal guardian is required to process personal data of children under 14, and a privacy policy is required when collecting personal data.
- The current list of designated self-rating operators was not retrieved (a 2021 list had nine, including Google, Apple, Samsung and Epic Games Korea).

## 5. Business setup, tax and in-app payment fees

### Takeaway
A Korean individual can open a Steamworks account with a W-8BEN, but in practice selling a rated game in Korea calls for 사업자등록 plus 게임제작업 registration, and in-app purchases call for 통신판매업 신고; overseas-platform revenue received in foreign currency is generally reported as zero-rated for VAT and as business income for 종합소득세. Store fees are in flux: Google's cut (30% to a 10-25% range) reaches Korea at the end of December 2026, while Apple still charges up to 30%, or 26% for third-party payment.

### Cited Findings
**Registration**
- Games that need a rating (PC/console) require 게임제작업 registration; it is filed with the local district office via 정부24, individuals are eligible, there is no registration fee but an annual registration licence tax applies — [게임메카](https://www.gamemeca.com/view.php?gid=1751893); [NEONVOID blog, 2026-10](https://icenovel.com/projects/neonvoid/neonvoid-steam-registration)
- Stove Indie's creator site carries a guide on how an indie developer should handle 사업자등록 — [Stove Indie for Creators](http://forcreators.stoveindie.com/post/?bmode=view&idx=84443359)
- 통신판매업 신고 is needed when a game sells in-app purchases; a solo-developer guide lists the order as 사업자등록, then 통신판매업 신고, then Google Play developer sign-up — [퇴근후서버다운 blog](https://blog.sidnft.com/815)
- An individual business applying for a rating must submit a distributor (배급업자) registration certificate issued by the local government (GRAC guidance, 2020, older) — [이데일리](https://www.edaily.co.kr/news/read?newsId=04414886622386256&mediaCodeNo=257)
- Conflicting older advice: a community answer of roughly seven years ago says a developer releasing only on Steam can sell without game-business registration — [inditor](https://inditor.co.kr/bbs/board.php?bo_table=str&wr_id=22)

**Steam payouts and US withholding**
- Steam charges USD 100 per game and requires tax information before release. Non-US persons are taxed on US-source income, in most cases at 30%; a tax treaty can reduce the rate, and W-8BEN must be completed to claim it — [Steamworks tax FAQ](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/finance/taxfaq?l=koreana)
- Individuals file W-8BEN and entities file W-8BEN-E; a W-8BEN generally stays valid until the end of the third calendar year after signing unless details change — [Deel](https://www.deel.com/ko-kr/blog/tax-forms/)
- US tax withheld above the treaty rate is not creditable in Korea and must be reclaimed through the US process (corporate example) — [위키독스](https://wikidocs.net/446456)

**Korean VAT and income tax**
- Revenue from an overseas platform operator received in foreign currency is generally treated as a service supplied to a foreign business and zero-rated (영세율) for VAT; the return must still be filed, input VAT can be refunded, and proof such as contracts, invoices and foreign-currency deposit records is needed. If the platform settles in KRW, zero-rating may be hard to apply (tax blog and Q&A sources about AdSense-type income, not game-specific) — [위키독스 blog, 2026](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@Insight_Lab/29318/); [택슬리 Q&A](https://taxly.kr/qna/133932-중개-무역에-따른-외화-입금에-대한-세금-신고-문의)
- Such income is business income for a Korean resident and is reported in the May 종합소득세 return together with any salary; overseas payers such as Google and Apple do not withhold Korean tax — [위키독스 blog, 2026](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@Insight_Lab/29318/)

**In-app payment law and store fees in Korea**
- Apple allowed third-party payment in Korea to comply with the Telecommunications Business Act amendment and set its commission at 26%, four points below its top in-app rate (2022, older) — [이코노미스트](https://economist.co.kr/article/view/ecn202207030017)
- On 2026-03-05 Google announced a cut in Play fees from 30% to at most 20%, with 15% for developers in its programs, separation of the service fee from the payment-processing fee, and permission for developers' own payment systems and external web payment links — [뉴시스 2026-03-05](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260305_0003535959); [CEO스코어데일리 2026-03-06](https://www.ceoscoredaily.com/page/view/2026030614294922535)

- On 2026-06-18, 26 industry and civic groups held a press conference saying Korea still pays 30%, and 26% even on external payment, and demanded cuts — [머니투데이 2026-06-18](https://www.mt.co.kr/article/2026061812375249948)

### Inferences
- Registering as an individual business (개인사업자) before launch is the low-risk setup: it enables 게임제작업 registration, rating applications, 통신판매업 신고, grant eligibility (most programs require a registered business or a team) and input-VAT refunds on outsourcing and equipment.
- A premium game sold only on Steam needs no 통신판매업 신고 under the logic above, because Valve is the seller of record; this is my reading and was not confirmed by an official source.
- Net take after store fee: Steam 70% everywhere; Google Play Korea should improve from late December 2026; ONE store is 80-95%. For a first title with small revenue, fee differences matter less than discoverability.

### Takeaway
KOCCA replaced its 2025 indie grant (KRW 3.5 billion, about 53 projects) with the 2026 "Korea Indie Game Dev Camp", a KRW 6 billion four-stage competition that drew 1,461 teams for about 20 final slots; festivals (BIC, IndieCraft, G-STAR) and Smilegate programs are the realistic entry points for a first-timer, and Google's 창구 gives up to KRW 200 million but only to mobile developers.

## 8. Korean freelance art and animation market

### Takeaway
Listed prices on 크몽 put a commercial-use character illustration at roughly KRW 50,000-400,000 and a simple Spine animation clip from about KRW 66,000, with studios quoting around KRW 300,000 per person-day; these are asking prices from individual sellers, not verified transaction averages.

### Cited Findings
- 크몽 game/character illustration listing: simple bust or half-body sketch/colour from KRW 55,000; full-body semi-realistic KRW 300,000; semi-realistic illustration with background KRW 400,000 — [크몽](https://kmong.com/gig/194537)
- A higher-end 크몽 game character illustration listing starts at KRW 309,000, tiered from bust to full body — [크몽](https://kmong.com/gig/290382)
- In-game casual character/monster concept art prepared for Spine starts at KRW 250,000 — [크몽](https://kmong.com/gig/523192)
- Spine-ready source art is listed with a minimum of KRW 400,000, negotiable by difficulty — [크몽](https://kmong.com/gig/532584)
- Spine animation plus Unity setup: one simple clip KRW 66,000, with higher packages at KRW 132,000 and KRW 264,000; complex characters and cutscenes are quoted per person-day — [크몽](https://kmong.com/gig/693116)
- One studio listing quotes KRW 300,000 per person per day times working days (e.g. three days is KRW 900,000 including VAT); Spine work is usually billed per clip (idle, attack, run, skill), with extra rigging cost for many parts and extra charges for concept changes — [크몽](https://kmong.com/gig/715237) (attribution uncertain)
- A 3D game character request example on 크몽's price guide lists a budget of about KRW 2 million including rigging and five basic animations — [크몽 price guide](https://kmong.com/prices/3d-%EA%B2%8C%EC%9E%84-%EC%86%8C%EC%8A%A4)
- Typical illustration packages specify one subject, two drafts, 7-14 working days and 3-5 revisions, with commercial use allowed — [크몽](https://kmong.com/gig/480547) (attribution uncertain)

### Inferences
- For a 2D game with, say, 10 characters each needing concept art plus 5-8 Spine clips, listed prices imply roughly KRW 0.6-1.0 million per character, so KRW 6-10 million for the cast before backgrounds, UI and effects. This is my arithmetic from asking prices and should be treated as an order-of-magnitude estimate.
- Outsourcing invoices from Korean freelancers registered as businesses carry 10% VAT, which a registered developer can reclaim; that is another reason to hold 사업자등록.
- Contracts should fix the commercial-use/copyright transfer scope, number of revisions, source-file delivery (PSD with separated parts, Spine project) and per-clip pricing in advance, since listings charge extra for concept changes and part counts.
